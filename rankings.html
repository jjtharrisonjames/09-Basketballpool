WHAT IS A ☆GEM?

Hidden Gem = teams likely to pile up 20+ wins but ranked outside the national top 25, usually in weaker conferences. These are your highest-leverage late-round draft picks.

Why it matters for your pool: With ~15 drafters taking 12 teams each (180 total), the top 25 go fast and everyone gets similar wins from them. A team like Grand Canyon in the WAC can win 25–27 games against weaker opponents and still be available in round 8+. You get elite win production at a bargain draft slot.

Scoring scale (0–10):

ScoreMeaningExample
0Top-25 team — no gem value, everyone drafts them earlyDuke, Florida, Arizona
5–6Decent mid-major, 18–20 projected winsDavidson, St. Bonaventure
7–8Strong mid-major, 22–24 projected wins in a weak conferenceSaint Mary's, VCU, San Diego State
9–10Elite mid-major, analytics darling, 25+ projected winsGrand Canyon, Miami OH, Drake, Utah State

Teams scoring 8+ get the ☆GEM tag next to their name. Draft strategy: let other players chase name recognition early, then stack 2–3 GEMs in rounds 6–10 to pad your win total.

WHAT IS ♦PORTAL?

Transfer Portal Class = ranking of the 2026 transfer portal haul. In modern college basketball, portal transfers move more impactful talent than high school recruiting. Teams with top portal classes often leap multiple tiers in a single off-season.
Teams scoring 8+ get the ♦PORTAL tag. 2026 leaders: Tennessee, Louisville, Texas, Indiana, Xavier, Miami, BYU, USC.
